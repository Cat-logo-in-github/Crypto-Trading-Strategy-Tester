A METHOD, NOT A PROMISE

The interesting part
is what survives
the test.

Good research leaves a trail you can follow. Every result should help you understand the question, the assumptions and the next experiment.

Read the methodology ↗
  1. 01

    Make the question precise.

    Review instruments, features, rules, sizing and execution assumptions before running.

  2. 02

    Respect what was knowable.

    Keep availability timestamps visible. Use completed information and later fills.

  3. 03

    Compare on the same terms.

    Use compatible capital, periods and costs. Reserve held-out data for evaluation.

  4. 04

    Keep the record you choose.

    Run in session memory by default. Explicit local saves and exports keep data identity, assumptions, warnings and results.

RESEARCH WITH OPEN EYES

Synthetic examples explain the software. Historical results can overfit. Predictive accuracy is not profitability, correlation is not causation, and no trading edge is promised. A catalog match can be a proxy, not the exact measurement you need. Requests originate on your computer; runs stay in RAM unless you choose local saving or export. Selected providers retain their own access, privacy and licensing terms. There is no real-money execution.
