PREDICT. THEN TEST.

Give your model a fair test.

Train on the past, choose settings on validation, and evaluate on a separate final period.

Supervised research

Uses the same imported market data and execution assumptions as Strategy research. CPU-only ridge regression is the supported model in this release.

Trailing input features
CHRONOLOGICAL BY DESIGN

A model never chooses itself on the final test.

TRAIN 60%VALIDATE 20%TEST 20%

Scaling is fitted on the training period. Labels that overlap a later period are removed. The best regularization value is chosen using validation error.

The final prediction target is a future close-to-close return. Trading decisions still fill at the next available open, with fees and slippage.

Prediction accuracy and investment return answer different questions. Neither establishes a reliable trading edge.

Model coefficients are saved as plain JSON. No executable model files are loaded. GPU training and deep models are future extensions.

Random feature subsets

Fits small ridge models using the current market dataset. Each trial uses one randomly selected subset and a fixed regularization value.

Repeated searches can overfit validation. Reserve fresh future data. Feature associations do not establish causality.

CSV contracts

Market timestamps are interval open times with explicit timezones. Set bar duration to the actual data interval. Each row must represent a completed bar.

timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume

External observations have their own observation and publication times. Missing or expired values do not satisfy a rule.

observed_at,available_at,value,field,location

Duplicate market timestamps, ambiguous event revisions and non-finite numbers are rejected. CSV imports do not normalize naive timezones or fill missing market bars.
